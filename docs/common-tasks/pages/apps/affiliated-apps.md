# Affiliated Apps

**Address:** **https://openms.de/affiliated-apps/**  ·  **Linked from:** top menu → Apps → Affiliated Apps

Projects from partners and the community that use OpenMS.

## What you can change

| Part of the page | What you can change | Where it is | How |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------|-----|
| Big title and short text at the top | The words | the page file `content/en/affiliated-apps.md` | [Edit the page file](../../edit-page-text.md#how-to-do-it) |
| The list of projects | Add, change or remove a project | `config.yaml` → `affiliateProjects` | [Affiliated Apps](../../update-affiliated-apps.md) |
| Heading and introduction | The words | `config.yaml` → `affiliateSection` | [Affiliated Apps](../../update-affiliated-apps.md) |

## How to make the change

- Words in **`config.yaml`**: open the file, search for the key word from the table (for example `affiliateProjects`), change only the words after the colon. Steps: [edit `config.yaml`](../../edit-homepage-text.md#how-to-change-any-block).
- Words in the **page file** (`content/en/…`): [edit the page file](../../edit-page-text.md#how-to-do-it).
