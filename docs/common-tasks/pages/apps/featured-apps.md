# Featured Apps

**Address:** **https://openms.de/featured-apps/**  ·  **Linked from:** top menu → Apps → Featured Apps

The apps the OpenMS team looks after. The same list also shows on the home page.

## What you can change

| Part of the page | What you can change | Where it is | How |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------|-----|
| Big title and short text at the top | The words | the page file `content/en/featured-apps.md` | [Edit the page file](../../edit-page-text.md#how-to-do-it) |
| The list of apps | Add, change or remove an app (name, text, logo, link) | `config.yaml` → `webapps` | [Featured Apps](../../update-featured-apps.md) |
| Heading text and "Contact us" note | The words | `config.yaml` → `webappsSection` | [Featured Apps](../../update-featured-apps.md) |

## How to make the change

- Words in **`config.yaml`**: open the file, search for the key word from the table (for example `webapps`), change only the words after the colon. Steps: [edit `config.yaml`](../../edit-homepage-text.md#how-to-change-any-block).
- Words in the **page file** (`content/en/…`): [edit the page file](../../edit-page-text.md#how-to-do-it).

## Leave these to the web team

- How the page looks: `assets/css/overrides/featured-apps.css`.
