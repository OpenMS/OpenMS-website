# Leadership

**Address:** **https://openms.de/contributors/**  ·  **Linked from:** top menu → About → Leadership; footer → ABOUT

The board of directors and the Executive Committee. Note: the address is `/contributors/`, not `/leadership/`.

## What you can change

| Part of the page | What you can change | Where it is | How |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------|-----|
| Names, roles and short texts | The words | `layouts/partials/contributors-leadership.html`, `contributors-exec.html` | Ask the web team ([who?](../../../workflow/who-to-ask.md)) |
| Photos | Add or replace a photo | `static/images/leadership_photos/` | [Add images](../../add-images.md) |

## How to make the change

- Words in **`layouts/`**: these are part of the design. Ask the web team ([who to ask](../../../workflow/who-to-ask.md)).

## Leave these to the web team

- Adding a person also needs a change in the design file. How the page looks: `assets/css/overrides/contributors.css`.
