# Privacy

**Address:** **https://openms.de/privacy/**  ·  **Linked from:** footer → LEGAL

The privacy policy. Its sections are built from the headings of the page file.

## What you can change

| Part of the page | What you can change | Where it is | How |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------|-----|
| Sections and all their text | The words | the page file `content/en/privacy.md` | [Change the text of a page](../../edit-page-text.md) |
| Short summary of each section | The words | `layouts/partials/privacy-main.html` | Ask the web team ([who?](../../../workflow/who-to-ask.md)) |

Keep the lines that start with `###` and `####` exactly as they are: the page is built from them.

## How to make the change

- Words in the **page file** (`content/en/…`): [edit the page file](../../edit-page-text.md#how-to-do-it).
- Words in **`layouts/`**: these are part of the design. Ask the web team ([who to ask](../../../workflow/who-to-ask.md)).

## Leave these to the web team

- How the page looks: `assets/css/overrides/privacy.css`.
