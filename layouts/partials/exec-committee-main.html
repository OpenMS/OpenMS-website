Executive Committee

The primary governance body of the OpenMS project. It guides technical direction, long-term sustainability, and community governance.

How the committee works See members

The committee is composed of the core developers of the project and is led by an Executive Chairperson elected from among its members.

Scope

Responsibilities

The Executive Committee is responsible for the direction, continuity, and reputation of the OpenMS project.

Direction and continuity

Determine the mission, direction, and vision of OpenMS. Plan for long-term continuity and direct development resources in line with that vision.

Bylaws and reputation

Maintain and update the project bylaws, and enhance the public image and reputation of OpenMS.

Committees

Form additional committees as needed.

Leadership

Elect the Executive Chairperson and manage the list of core developers.

Code of Conduct

Address Code of Conduct issues.

Membership

Composition

The Executive Committee consists of the core developers of OpenMS — active participants who help maintain the software, guide development, and support the community.

Adding core developers

New core developers may be added by a simple majority vote of a quorum of the Executive Committee.

Removal of core developers

Core developers may be removed due to inactivity or a Code of Conduct violation by a two-thirds majority vote of a quorum, or by request of the member.

Leadership

Executive Chairperson

The committee elects an Executive Chairperson from among its members for a one-year term. The chairperson may be removed by a two-thirds vote of the Executive Committee.

Convene meetings

Convene and chair meetings of the Executive Committee.

Give notice

Provide notice of meetings to committee members.

Break ties

Break tie votes when necessary.

Process

Meetings

The Executive Committee meets regularly to coordinate the governance and development of the project.

  1. Cadence

    The committee meets at least once per calendar month.

  2. Scheduling

    Meetings are scheduled by the Executive Chairperson, or by any committee member if the position is vacant.

  3. Notice

    Members must receive at least 48 hours notice of meetings.

  4. Quorum

    A quorum of five members is required for votes.

  5. Procedure

    Proceedings follow Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised if requested by any committee member.

Bylaws

Amendments

Any member of the Executive Committee may propose amendments to the project bylaws.

  1. Circulate

    A proposed amendment is circulated to all committee members.

  2. Discuss and vote

    The amendment is discussed and voted on at the next regular meeting.

  3. Adopt

    Adoption requires a two-thirds majority of the quorum.

Open science

Transparency

Minutes are recorded for meetings of governance committees and made publicly available unless the committee votes otherwise. This keeps OpenMS accountable to the community.
