Why sponsor OpenMS

Your sponsorship helps OpenMS build and maintain a unified, trusted, and openly accessible commons of knowledge, software, education, and research resources for the mass spectrometry community.

01

Your sponsorship enables

  • Continued development and maintenance of OpenMS software and infrastructure
  • Training programs, workshops, and educational materials
  • Community outreach and researcher support
  • Open standards, interoperability, and reproducible science

How sponsorship works

OpenMS Inc. is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity that stewards project finances and accepts sponsorships aligned with our nonprofit mission.

  • Organizational sponsorships and grants
  • In-kind support for infrastructure or services
  • Co-branded community initiatives where appropriate

What is OpenMS’s tax identification number?

OpenMS’s tax identification number is 93-3818533.

Can I just make a one-time donation?

OpenMS appreciates donations of any size and, as we are a US registered 501(c)(3) corporation, your donation may be tax deductible. If you are interested in donating to OpenMS at a different level than sponsorship, see our donation page.

Who can I talk to about supporting OpenMS?

If you would like more information about supporting OpenMS, please contact ceo@openms.de.

How do sponsorship renewals happen?

Your sponsorship runs for one year after the date of receipt of payment. During this period, we display your logo on our Sponsors page. Two months before the end of your sponsorship, we will contact you requesting renewal. If renewal does not take place by the renewal date of the sponsorship, OpenMS may remove your logo from the thanks page.

How can I support OpenMS in other ways?

You can support OpenMS without making a financial contribution. For more information see our Getting Involved page.
