Structure

Two layers of governance

A nonprofit corporation provides legal and financial stewardship, while the Executive Committee leads the software project.

01

OpenMS Inc.

A U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit that holds the project’s legal entity, compliance, and financial continuity.

  • Board of Directors oversees fiduciary and legal duties
  • Supports fundraising, grants, and organizational continuity
See board of directors →
02

Executive Committee

Core developers who set the software roadmap, community policies, and day-to-day technical decisions.

  • Defines project direction and technical priorities
  • Maintains governance policies and core developer membership
  • Handles Code of Conduct escalations and process decisions
See executive committee →

Community

How Core Developers Fit In

Core developers are trusted maintainers who review contributions, mentor contributors, and participate in project governance through the Executive Committee.

Process

Membership

How people join and leave the core developer group.

Joining

New core developers are added by majority vote of the Executive Committee.

Leaving

Core developers may step down voluntarily or be removed for inactivity or Code of Conduct violations by a two-thirds vote.

Separation of Responsibilities
